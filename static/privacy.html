Zero-PII Architecture

Privacy Policy — {{APP_NAME}}

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Core Privacy Guarantee: Your Files Never Touch Our Servers

{{APP_NAME}} is engineered around a strict client-side, zero-retention architecture. When you inspect metadata, redact documents or images, or audit Shared Links across your Box folders, all file reading, WebAssembly processing, redaction rendering, and folder metadata scanning execute directly inside your web browser memory communicating over HTTPS with Box's official APIs (api.box.com and upload.box.com).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Store

  • We never download, proxy, log, or store your Box file contents on our servers.
  • We never store your name, email address, filenames, folder structures, or Shared Link URLs.
  • We never persist Box OAuth 2.0 access tokens or refresh tokens in any database; your short-lived session token lives exclusively in volatile browser memory until you close the modal.

3. Anonymous Quota & Subscription Accounting

To enforce monthly Free and Pro plan limits without storing personal data, our stateless Google Cloud Run service computes a one-way cryptographic hash (HMAC-SHA256) of your numeric Box account identifier and stores only that irreversible hash alongside an integer usage counter and plan tier (free or pro) in Google Cloud Firestore.

4. User-Owned Snapshots & Outputs

Any sanitized copies (_SafeCopy), exported CSV audit reports, or optional ShareRadar traffic-delta snapshots (_safeshare_radar_snapshot.json) are saved strictly inside your own Box account under your organization's governance and access controls.
