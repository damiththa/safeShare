Privacy Policy — {{APP_NAME}}

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Core Privacy Guarantee: 100% In-Browser Comparison

{{APP_NAME}} is engineered around a strict client-side, zero-retention architecture. When you compare Box file versions or sibling documents across PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx / .csv), text, or image formats, all file downloads, text redlining, visual pixel comparison, spreadsheet formula parsing, and PDF report generation execute exclusively inside your web browser memory over direct HTTPS connections with Box's official APIs (api.box.com and upload.box.com).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Store

  • We never download, proxy, log, or store your Box file contents or diff text on our servers.
  • We never store your name, email address, filenames, folder structures, or version comments.
  • We never persist Box OAuth 2.0 access tokens or refresh tokens in any database; your short-lived session token lives exclusively in volatile browser memory until you close the tab or modal.

3. Anonymous Quota & Subscription Accounting

To enforce monthly Free and Pro comparison limits without storing personal data, our stateless Google Cloud Run service computes a one-way domain-separated cryptographic hash (HMAC-SHA256) of your numeric Box account identifier and stores only that irreversible hash alongside an integer monthly comparison counter and plan tier (free or pro) in Google Cloud Firestore.

4. User-Owned Comparison Reports

Any exported Redline_Report.pdf summaries you choose to save back to Box are uploaded directly from your browser into your own Box folder under your organization's governance and access controls.
